# ⚡ HackJudge Participant Portal — Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

HackJudge Participant Portal exists to support a fair, respectful, and technically meaningful hackathon experience. We welcome contributors, organizers, participants, reviewers, and users of every experience level.

We are committed to collaboration built on respect, empathy, accountability, accessibility, and engineering integrity.

## Expected Behavior

Community members should:

- Communicate respectfully and professionally.
- Focus feedback on ideas, implementation, and evidence—not individuals.
- Welcome questions and different technical perspectives.
- Respect privacy, confidential repositories, credentials, and participant data.
- Disclose security vulnerabilities privately through `SECURITY.md`.
- Credit original work and respect third-party licenses.
- Report errors honestly and distinguish facts from assumptions.
- Avoid manipulating submissions, scores, leaderboard data, or event configuration.
- Help maintain an inclusive and accessible environment.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following is prohibited:

- Harassment, discrimination, hate speech, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Unwanted sexual attention or inappropriate personal conduct.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Exposing passwords, tokens, private source code, or participant information.
- Tampering with evaluation results, administrative controls, or audit-relevant data.
- Submitting malicious code, abusive requests, or intentionally harmful dependencies.
- Misrepresenting testing, evaluation, security findings, or contribution ownership.
- Retaliating against good-faith reporters or participants who raise concerns.

## Scope

This policy applies to the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, project-managed communication channels, and any setting where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer’s GitHub profile contact method. Include the relevant context, links, dates, and evidence when safe. Do not publicly disclose private or sensitive information.

Security vulnerabilities must be reported according to `SECURITY.md`, not through public issues.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may investigate reports and take proportionate action, including content removal, warnings, temporary restrictions, or permanent removal from project spaces. Serious security, privacy, or legal matters may be escalated to appropriate parties.

Maintainers should handle reports consistently, confidentially, and without retaliation. Enforcement decisions may consider context, impact, repetition, and willingness to correct the behavior.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Maintainers are expected to:

- Apply this policy in good faith and consistently.
- Protect reporters and affected individuals from retaliation.
- Avoid unnecessary disclosure of confidential information.
- Separate technical disagreement from personal misconduct.
- Document significant enforcement actions appropriately.

## Attribution

This policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant and adapted for the HackJudge Participant Portal’s competition, privacy, and engineering-integrity context.
